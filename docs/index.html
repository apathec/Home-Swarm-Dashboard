Swarm Dashboard

Bitcoin Home-Mining Swarm Monitor · ESP32

A self-contained, network-connected dashboard for monitoring your home Bitcoin miners on a 4" ESP32 touchscreen. Polls Bitaxe, NerdAxe, and Avalon Nano 3s on your LAN — and displays live hashrate, power, temperature, best difficulty, and uptime.

No subscriptions. No cloud. No telemetry.
Everything runs on the ESP32 you already paid for.

What it does

Everything you need, nothing you don't

⚡ Live Wave Polling

Miners are polled one at a time with a visible "scanning" highlight. Watch the data refresh in real time.

🌡️ Color-Coded Health

Temperatures turn yellow at 60°C and red at 70°C. Spot a hot miner across the room.

📊 Pool Aggregation

Combined hashrate, accepted/rejected counts, and most-common pool URL across your whole swarm.

🌐 Built-in Web UI

Add miners, change polling intervals, factory reset — all from your phone or computer.

📡 WiFi Captive Portal

First-boot setup. No hardcoded credentials, no recompiling.

💾 Survives Reboots

Miner list and settings are persisted to flash storage. Power-cycle without losing config.

Supported Miners

Mix and match your swarm

Bitaxe

Any model running AxeOS firmware. Polls hashrate, power, temp, difficulty, shares, uptime over HTTP.

NerdAxe

Multi-chip Bitaxe-derivatives. Same AxeOS API, same data fields. Plug-and-play.

Avalon Nano 3s

Direct CGMiner TCP connection. No cloud relay. Reads chip temp, real wattage, best difficulty.

Setup

From box to mining swarm in 5 steps

Plug In

Connect your HOSYOND 4" ESP32 board to any computer via USB-C.

Click Install

Open the install page in Chrome, click the install button, pick your USB device.

Connect WiFi

Board boots into a captive portal. Pick your home network and save.

Add Miners

Browse to the dashboard's IP. Add miner name, IP, and type. Done.

Watch Your Swarm

Live hashrate, temperatures, share counts. Sit back and admire.
